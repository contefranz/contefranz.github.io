Research

My research uses NLP, machine learning, and computational statistics on large-scale textual and multimodal financial data to study corporate disclosure and its consequences — investor reactions to layered narratives, firm tax outcomes, disclosure cascades, and the diagnostics of topic models. Below: published work, working papers, work in progress, books, and recent talks.
